## Direct answers to the six focus questions

### 1. Do the state-machine tests assert the *machine*, or just the endpoint's status code?

**The machine — and this is the strongest thing in the batch.** `OrderStateSourceTests`
(2590-2696) is a `SimpleTestCase`, no HTTP, no DB:

- `test_transition_allowed_pins_the_current_machine` (2610-2634) asserts **14 edges**: 4 legal,
  7 illegal (including both "skip a step" and "move backwards" and an unknown source), plus 5
  reflexive self-transitions. `state.py:58-61` is `new == old or new in ALLOWED.get(old, set())`,
  so this truth table pins the whole function, not a sample of it.
- `test_machine_constants_are_single_sourced` (2597-2608) uses `assertIs` on
  `admin.ALLOWED_TRANSITIONS`, `admin.transition_allowed`, `views.ALLOWED_TRANSITIONS`,
  `views.transition_allowed`, `ADMIN_FULFILMENT_NEXT` and `Order.STATUS_CHOICES`. Identity, not
  equality — a redefined copy anywhere reds. This is a genuine single-source-of-truth guard.
- `test_payment_transition_table_pins_the_retry_semantics` (2650-2677) pins the payment
  dimension the same way, including the two semantics that matter (`pending→failed`,
  `failed→captured`) and the two that must never exist (`captured→failed`, `pending→refunded`).

Endpoint tests then verify *writers obey* the machine: 2979-3005 (admin change form illegal move
moves nothing), 3317-3330 (illegal move appends no event), 3437-3504 (cancel sweep skips a row
that flipped). The fulfil seam's terminal-status 409 is covered by
`test_fulfill_rejects_terminal_states_in_the_envelope` at **2412-2423 — outside this range**, so I
do not report its absence here.

Net: the machine is pinned directly **and** at the writers. Only the *lock* under those writers
is unpinned (Q18-01).

### 2. Does the race-guard test actually run concurrency, or simulate it?

**It simulates it, and the simulation cannot see the lock it exists to protect (Q18-01).**

- `ApiTestCase` is `class ApiTestCase(TestCase)` (`common/testing.py:54`) — one connection, the
  whole test inside one wrapping transaction. No second connection, no thread, no
  `TransactionTestCase`.
- The "concurrent actor" is `Order.objects.filter(pk=victim.pk).update(status="cancelled")`
  fired from inside an overridden `values_list` (2552-2562), i.e. **before** the writer's
  `transaction.atomic()` opens and before `_locked_orders` takes `select_for_update`
  (`admin.py:568-599`).
- What the test therefore proves is `admin.py:630-631`: the per-row **re-read** disagrees with
  the stale snapshot and the row is skipped. That check is mutation-sensitive — delete
  `if order.status not in allowed_from: continue` and the victim is written back to `confirmed`,
  so 2568 reds. Credit where due: it also nails the audit cardinality (2575-2582: skipped row
  gets no event, swept row gets exactly one).
- What it cannot prove: `select_for_update()`. **Delete `.select_for_update()` from
  `_locked_orders` and both 2518 and 3437 stay green** — the in-process re-read is unaffected by
  a lock nobody contends. A grep of the whole backend finds zero tests exercising the lock: the
  real concurrency suite `tests/test_e2e_concurrency.py` covers verify-oversell, single-use
  coupon, and pagination only — not the admin bulk action. `tests.py:1161-1163` goes the other
  way and *mocks `select_for_update` out* to fake an interleave.
- The test is also coupled to the snapshot's implementation: if 2606's
  `.values_list("pk", flat=True)` became `.values("pk")` or `list(qs)`, the interleave silently
  never fires, the victim stays `pending`, gets swept, and the test reds **as a fixture error**
  wearing the costume of an invariant failure. Its name and docstring ("the bulk updater's
  UPDATE must re-apply the status__in predicate", 2512-2516) describe an `.update()` the sweep no
  longer uses — the per-row rework is acknowledged only in a trailing comment (2571).

### 3. Does the shipped-precondition test cover both halves, and does deleting either check red it?

**Both halves are enforced at two of the three writers, and deleting either check reds there.
The third writer's items half and the *aggregation* are unpinned (Q18-03, Q18-04).**

| Writer | payment legit | payment blocked | items legit | items blocked |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| bulk `mark_shipped` | 3704-3719 | **3653-3686** | 3704-3719 | **3688-3702** |
| change form | 3751-3763 | **3723-3735** | — | **3737-3749** |
| JSON `fulfill` seam | 3782-3790 | **3767-3780** | 3782-3790 | *none* |

Mutation proof (source-read): replacing `_require_items_to_ship`'s body with `return []`
(`models.py:519-524`) reds 3688, 3737 and 3813 — so the check cannot be deleted unnoticed
overall. But nothing pins it **at the seam**, and nothing pins the reason string at any writer
except the payment one (3683-3686 asserts `"payment must be captured"`; 3688-3702 asserts only
status + event absence, no warning text).

The real hole is the pair, not either half: **no fixture fails both preconditions**
(`_confirmed_row(paid=False, with_item=False)` is never constructed), so
`precondition_failures`' aggregation (`state.py:117-120`) and the admin's multi-reason join
(`admin.py:674-683` — the message that exists to show *all* unmet reasons at once) are exercised
with at most one reason. A latent `return failures[:1]` is green across all 13 tests. And 3819's
`assertNotEqual(order_state.precondition_failures(prepaid, "shipped"), [])` is content-blind: a
cross-wired check that answered the *items* message for an unpaid row passes it.

### 4. Do the reservation-lifecycle and cart-clear tests assert money/stock, or only row counts?

**Reservation lifecycle: genuinely money/stock.** Not one of the 16 is a row-count test.

- `test_verify_converts_active_holds_into_committed_sales` (4283-4312): `product.stock == 8`,
  a `StockMovement` with `delta=-2`, `reason=SALE`, `note=f"Order #{order.id}"`, hold `CONVERTED`,
  and the replay 400 leaves exactly one `CONVERTED` hold.
- `test_failed_verify_releases_holds_and_the_retry_rechecks_cleanly` (4350-4383): stock
  **10** after the failure (nothing was taken) and **8** after the retry — i.e. decremented
  exactly once across a failure/retry pair, with the released hold never resurrected.
- `test_stock_conflict_verify_releases_holds_and_retry_rechecks` (4385-4415): stock 10→1,
  409, stock restored, retry → 8. `test_stock_unavailable_handler_retry_after_restock_captures`
  (4500-4526): stock 6 (`10-2-2`), `StockMovement.objects.count() == 2` (one row per line),
  `payment_status == "captured"`, `paid_at` set.
- `test_stock_unavailable_handler_answers_the_wire_envelope` (4438-4460) is the **only test in
  the batch that asserts the rendered wire** (`res.content` byte-for-byte, 4450-4453) rather
  than `res.data`, and it proves the summed `requested=4` came from the service's conflict rather
  than the view's per-line `requested=2`.
- `test_stock_unavailable_handler_half_applies_nothing_and_releases_holds` (4462-4498) is a real
  partial-failure test: stock unchanged at 3, `StockMovement` count unchanged, no event, no
  `paid_at`, `razorpay_payment_id` NULL, hold `RELEASED`.

**Cart capture/clear: stock yes, ordering no.** Stock is asserted everywhere (4723, 4744, 4772,
4854) and the replay test pins `StockMovement.objects.count() == 1` (4745). What is *not*
asserted is the ordering claim the class exists for — see Q18-05.

## What these tests do prove (credit, so the batch is not read as uniformly weak)

1. **The machine is pinned twice** — directly (2610-2696) and at the writers (2979, 3317, 3437).
2. **Per-row re-check is mutation-proof** in both bulk writers (`admin.py:630`, `794`): deleting
   either `continue` reds 2568 / 3493.
3. **The unique-constraint retarget is real, not mocked** (4255-4279): the `IntegrityError`
   fallback is reached through the actual `(order, product)` constraint, and the re-armed hold is
   asserted for quantity, status and TTL.
4. **Env-driven TTL is proven as env-driven** (4219-4237): `override_settings(RESERVATION_TTL=120)`
   plus a `before/after` window, so the value is read at call time, not at import.
5. **The concurrency *consequences* on the money path are asserted, not just codes**: stock,
   `StockMovement` count/`delta`/`reason`/`note`, hold status transitions, and the exact wire
   envelope (4450-4453).
6. **The webhook path is exercised for real** (4790-4858): HMAC over the actual body, real
   signature header, `PaymentEvent.Outcome.APPLIED`, cart emptied, stock decremented — the one
   end-to-end proof of the reported phantom-cart bug.
7. **Negative/authz cases exist where they matter most**: guest checkout refused and links nothing
   (4862-4874), unlinked order captures harmlessly (4748-4774), `SET_NULL` keeps the cart
   (4778-4786), out-of-set rows never swept (2518), no-op and illegal admin saves append nothing
   (3317), view-only event admin including the superuser bypass (3517-3551).
