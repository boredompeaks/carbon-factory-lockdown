## Direct answers to the three focus questions

1. **Does the deployment-contract test assert the artifact against what the app needs, or grep for
   strings?** Both, and the split is not where the docstrings claim it is. The three classes that
   *boot* the app (`BuildLayerSatisfiesTheNonDebugGuardsTests`, `TlsHardeningDeployContractTests`,
   `ComposeRuntimeEnvContractTests`) are genuinely behavioural: they parse the env out of the
   Dockerfile/compose and then run `manage check`, `collectstatic`, `migrate` and a `django.test.Client`
   probe in a subprocess, with a negative control that drops a guard key and asserts the boot is still
   refused (`:574-580`). Those are the strongest pins in the flow. Everything about the *release
   sequence* — the thing SPEC-22-07 actually delivers — is string matching over a hand-built file list
   (`:318-411`), and it is green while the script `deploy.yml` really executes is a different file
   (`scripts/release-uat.sh`) that the list does not contain and the regex cannot match (Q28-02).
   The single-migrate and never-master pins are also structurally unable to see the shapes they exist
   to catch (Q28-02, Q28-04).
2. **Does the throttle test prove two processes share a budget, or only that a setting is a
   filebased backend?** It proves sharing (an OS process is genuinely spawned and the parent's
   remaining allowance is `BUDGET-2`, which is discriminating — a separate cache would leave
   `BUDGET-1`), but only *when `REDIS_URL` is exported in the test environment*; otherwise both
   behavioural pins `skipTest`. What no test in the repo proves is the deployment wiring: `compose`
   gives `REDIS_URL` to `worker` and `beat` and deliberately not to `backend`, and the image CMD runs
   `--workers 3`, so the shipped coupon budget is 3×30/min. That gap is already tracked as F3-C4 /
   OWNER-BRIEF B2 (Q28-08, KNOWN). The file-local claims that are *not* shared-cache proofs are
   Q28-07.
3. **Does the email-probe test prove the probe refuses real SMTP?** No, and it cannot as written.
   It proves the probe *does* send when the backend is SMTP (faked) and that it refuses the three
   backends the command's own blacklist names — the citation loops over exactly
   `NON_DELIVERING_BACKENDS` and omits `filebased` (Q28-09, the test-side half of QC-104). One test
   (`:186-190`) is not inside the `smtplib.SMTP` patch at all, so its hermeticity rests on the very
   allowlist guard it is testing (Q28-10).
